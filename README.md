## The entry

> I watered the plastic plant on the windowsill every morning for a month and let the real one die of thirst.
> I now check whether something is actually alive before I care for it, and I start with the plant on the windowsill.

`main.py` writes these two lines to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, every run.
